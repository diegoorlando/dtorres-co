Education

Diego has both a bachelor's and master's degrees in mechanical engineering from the Georgia Institute of Technology (Georgia Tech), where he also studied music and Spanish.

At Georgia Tech, Diego conducted research with the Microelecetronics Packaging Research Center in Atlanta, GA, USA, and the Ultrasonics Laboratory in Metz, France.

Diego also was part of the Society of Hispanic Professional Engineers, the American Society of Mechanical Engineers, Pi Tau Sigma ****, the Georgia Tech Symphony Orchestra, Chorale, and Symphonic Band.
