Music

Diego started his musical training in the Colombian System of National Orchestras (Batuta), where he started playing the (French) horn and sang. He continued his horn studies at the Palm Beach State College with Susan Kynkor and Pedja Ivanovich, where he played with the Symphonic Band, and in Los Angeles with Annie Bosler and Dylan Skyhart.

He has also played with the Georgia Tech Symphony Orchestra, the Georgia Tech Symphonic Band, the Playa Pops, the Black Rock Philharmonic , and the Symphonic Project of the Autonomous University of Bucaramanga.

He also sang bass with the Georgia Tech Chorale.
