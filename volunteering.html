Diego started volunteering as an undergraduate student with the Hispanic Scholarship Fund and the Society of Hispanic Professional Engineers, where he tutored children and teenagers in under priviledged areas in Atlanta, GA. Diego focused mainly in math and sciences.

In 2026, Diego cofounds the Organization for the Development of Santander , a Florida based non-profit that focuses on educational and technological initiatives for under priviledged children and adolescents in Bucaramanga, Colombia, with the purpose to offer high quality education opportunities for people who otherwise would not be able to access them. .
