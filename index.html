About Diego Torres:

Diego Torres is a mechanical engineer and amateur musician from Bucaramanga, Colombia. He confounded and is the President of the Organization for Santander's Developement, a non-profit focused on educational and technology initiatives in Santander, Colombia, and is currently on the Board of Directors of the Black Rock Philharmonic.

He was the founding/chief engineer of Sorting Robotics (Y Combinator 2019) and was an early employee of Verdigris Tech.

He plays the French horn, plays soccer, and is an avid traveller.
